Is a Financial Digital Twin worth building, and how much of one?

FinTwin mirrors a manufacturing SME's machines, inventory and invoices day by day, turns the physical state into financial state (CCC, working capital, credit score, loan rate, failure cost) and then prices the twin itself with an overhead-aware, Monte-Carlo capital-budgeting model.

Research gap addressed

Our CIE-1 survey of 11 IEEE/ACM papers found that financial digital twins are justified with static, deterministic NPV (Survey Sec. 5) while the literature itself warns that their computational, energy and integration overheads can erode the value they create (Gap 2, Yoon et al. [A7]) and that results are validated only in simulation (Gap 4).

  1. Gap 2 → price the twin's own hidden overheads (edge energy [A1], hardware refresh, compliance, ramp-up) and right-size its fidelity.
  2. Eq. 4–5 → replace static NPV with Monte-Carlo NPV discounted at a volatility-adjusted rate r(σ).
  3. Gap 4 → train the failure model on the real AI4I 2020 dataset and measure benefits from a twin simulation instead of assuming them.

Module 04

How much twin is worth building?

Each fidelity tier captures more benefit but costs more capital, energy, refresh and compliance. FinTwin picks the tier with the highest risk-adjusted NPV subject to a loss-probability limit, and flags decision reversals where static NPV says "accept" but the risk-aware model says "reject".
